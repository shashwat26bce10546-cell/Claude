## Description

Aap roz ₹ use karte ho… par kya aap jaante ho isse banaya kisne? 🤔

2010 tak Indian rupee ka apna koi symbol hi nahi tha — dollar ka $ tha, pound ka £ tha, aur hum bas "Rs." likhte the. Phir sarkaar ne poore desh mein ek contest rakha, aur 3000 se zyada designs aaye.

Jeeta IIT Bombay ka ek young PhD student — D. Udaya Kumar! ✏️
Unhone Devanagari ka "र" aur English ka "R" mila diya, aur upar do lines jodi — tirange ki nishaani aur barabari (equal to) ka sign.

15 July 2010 ko Union Cabinet ne unka design approve kiya. Aur twist? Lagbhag usi samay woh IIT Guwahati mein professor ki nayi naukri shuru kar rahe the! 🎓

Agli baar ₹ dekho, toh Udaya Kumar ko yaad karna. 🇮🇳
