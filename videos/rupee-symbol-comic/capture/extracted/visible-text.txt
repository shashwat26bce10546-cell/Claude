Topic: Who designed the Indian rupee symbol (₹)?
- Before 2010 the Indian rupee had no symbol of its own; prices were written "Rs". The dollar, pound, euro and yen had symbols.
- In 2009 the Government of India ran an open nationwide competition for a rupee symbol; over 3,000 entries (reported 3,331) were received; five were shortlisted.
- The winning design came from D. Udaya Kumar, then a PhD student at IIT Bombay (Industrial Design Centre).
- The design blends the Devanagari letter "र" (Ra) with the Roman capital "R"; two parallel lines at the top allude to the tricolour and also form an "equal to" sign, symbolising the nation's desire to reduce economic disparity.
- The Union Cabinet approved the symbol on 15 July 2010.
