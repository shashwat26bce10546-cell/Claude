## 2. Consistency sheet (repeat in EVERY prompt)

- **Chandragupta (young):** lean, determined face, short black beard, bronze armor over cream dhoti, simple gold armlet, spear.
- **Chanakya:** thin older man, shaved head with a tuft, white robe, wooden staff, sharp eyes.
- **Ashoka (mature):** broad face, trimmed beard, rich red and gold royal robe, tall gold crown, calm heavy eyes.
- **Setting:** 3rd century BCE Magadha, Pataliputra with wooden palisades, stone and wood palaces, Ganga river.
- **Palette:** warm gold, deep red, dusty brown, soft sunset light.
