# ₹ Symbol Kisne Banaya? — 3D Short Script

Format modelled on *"Gandhiji's Photo on Currency Notes (3D)"* by Professor Of How
(https://youtube.com/shorts/LdlVXPXYsD0).

- **Canvas:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920
- **Length:** ~55 sec
- **Language:** Hinglish voiceover (casual storyteller tone, slight suspense)
- **Look:** Stylised 3D characters (game-cinematic, soft daylight, clean interiors)

---

## 1. Reference breakdown (what makes the format work)

| Element | What the original does | Rule to copy |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0–3s) | Gandhi holds a ₹500 note and points at his own face on it | Open on the **payoff object** plus a character reacting to it. Shows the end first |
| Narrative | A hidden person (the photographer) never imagined his photo would matter ("sapne mein bhi…") | Centre the story on an **unknown person behind a famous thing** |
| Staging | 3D characters in a palace, guards, a British official, a red arrow on the key person | Short scenes, one action each. **Red arrow / circle** to point out the hero in a crowd |
| Proof beat | Real B&W photo next to the note portrait, big red **"1949"** | Put the **real artifact next to the 3D version**, with the year in large red type |
| Twist | "phir bhi notes par Ashoka ka sher chuna gaya" | Add a turn the viewer doesn't expect before the final payoff |
| Captions | White Roman-script Hinglish, lower-middle, 2 lines max | Same. Key years / numbers in **red, oversized** |
| Ending | Back to the object everyone knows | Loop back to the hook image so the Short **replays seamlessly** |

## 2. Script

**Story:** In 2010 the government ran an open competition for a symbol for the Indian rupee.
D. Udaya Kumar, a PhD student at IIT Bombay, beat thousands of entries. His design,
the Devanagari "र" merged with the Roman "R" with two horizontal lines on top, was approved by the
Union Cabinet on **15 July 2010**. Around the same day he started his new job as an
assistant professor at IIT Guwahati.

> ⚠️ Check these facts against a primary source (e.g. PIB press release, July 2010) before publishing:
> the exact number of entries (reported as ~3,000+), the prize amount (reported as ₹2.5 lakh),
> and the "same day he joined IIT Guwahati" detail.

## 3. Caption style

- Font: bold rounded sans (e.g. Poppins SemiBold), white, soft black shadow
- Position: lower-middle (≈ 62–68% from top), clear of the YouTube Shorts UI
- Max 2 lines, 3–5 words per line, timed to the VO
- Years and numbers (**2009, 3000+, 15 July 2010**) get their own big red text card (#E53935)
- Red arrow / circle pointer: 6–8 frames pop-in with a slight overshoot, held 1.5s

## 4. Audio

- Music: light, curious pizzicato or lo-fi "mystery" bed, low under the VO
- SFX: paper whoosh (sc 3), pencil scratch (sc 5), easel thud as each one dims (sc 6), "boom" hit on the year card (sc 7), phone buzz (sc 8)
- The final line ends on a soft "ding" that matches the hook, so the loop feels seamless
