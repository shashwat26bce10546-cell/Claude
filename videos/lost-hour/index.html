SOMETHING CAME BACK
NO ONE REMEMBERS
In Hollow Creek, nothing ever happened.
Every night was quiet. Every road was empty.
Until the night a light appeared above the trees.
And at exactly three seventeen A.M.
every clock in town stopped. For one hour.
Four hundred and twelve people vanished from their homes.
When the hour ended, they came back. Exactly where they'd been.
But something came back with them.
Doctors found nothing. Police found nothing.
And no one can remember where they went.
Now the light has returned.
